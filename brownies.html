Brownies

A rich, chewy brownie with a crisp edge and a soft center.

Prep Time

15 minutes

Cook Time

30 minutes

Serves

12 squares

Ingredients

Ingredient Amount
Unsalted butter 1/2 cup
Dark chocolate 4 ounces
Granulated sugar 1 cup
Brown sugar 1/2 cup
Eggs 2 large
Vanilla extract 1 teaspoon
Flour 3/4 cup
Cocoa powder 1/4 cup

Directions

  1. Preheat the oven to 350°F and line an 8x8 baking pan with parchment paper.
  2. Melt the butter and dark chocolate together in a heatproof bowl, then let it cool for a few minutes.
  3. Whisk in the granulated sugar, brown sugar, eggs, and vanilla until smooth.
  4. Fold in the flour and cocoa powder until just combined.
  5. Pour the batter into the prepared pan and spread it evenly.
  6. Bake for 25 to 30 minutes, or until the center is set and the edges look slightly firm.
  7. Cool completely before cutting into squares and serving.
