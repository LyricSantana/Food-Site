Classic Grilled Cheese

Golden, buttery bread with melty cheddar in the middle.

Prep Time

10 minutes

Cook Time

10 minutes

Serves

2 sandwiches

Ingredients

Ingredient Amount
Sourdough bread 4 slices
Cheddar cheese 4 slices
Butter 2 tablespoons
Mayonnaise 1 tablespoon
Garlic powder 1/4 teaspoon
Salt Pinch

Directions

  1. Mix the softened butter, mayonnaise, and garlic powder in a small bowl.
  2. Spread the mixture on one side of each bread slice.
  3. Layer cheddar between the unbuttered sides of the bread.
  4. Heat a skillet over medium heat and place the sandwich inside.
  5. Cook each side for 2 to 4 minutes until the bread is golden and the cheese is melted.
  6. Remove from the pan, slice in half, and serve warm.
