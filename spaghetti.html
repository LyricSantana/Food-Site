Easy Spaghetti

A quick and comforting pasta dinner with a simple tomato sauce.

Prep Time

10 minutes

Cook Time

20 minutes

Serves

4 people

Ingredients

Ingredient Amount
Spaghetti 1 pound
Olive oil 2 tablespoons
Garlic 3 cloves
Crushed tomatoes 1 can
Tomato paste 2 tablespoons
Salt 1 teaspoon
Black pepper 1/2 teaspoon
Parmesan 1/2 cup

Directions

  1. Boil a large pot of salted water and cook the spaghetti until al dente.
  2. Heat olive oil in a skillet and cook the minced garlic until fragrant.
  3. Stir in the tomato paste, then add the crushed tomatoes, salt, and pepper.
  4. Let the sauce simmer for 10 to 15 minutes until thickened.
  5. Drain the pasta and reserve a little pasta water.
  6. Toss the spaghetti with the sauce, adding a splash of pasta water if needed.
  7. Serve topped with Parmesan and extra black pepper.
